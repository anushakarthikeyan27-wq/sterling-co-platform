Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, password (stored securely, never in plain text), and optionally your location. If you're a professional, we also store your bio, portfolio details, and credentials that you choose to add.

How we use it

Your information is used to operate your account, connect you with clients or professionals, display your public profile (if you're a professional), and send you notifications about messages, project requests, and reviews.

Local storage, not cookies

Sterling & Co uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits. We do not use tracking cookies or share data with third-party advertisers.

Contact information

A professional's phone number and email are kept private and are not shown publicly until a client's project request has been accepted.

Data storage

Your data is stored in a MongoDB Atlas database. We take reasonable steps to protect it, including password hashing, but as with any online service, no method of storage is 100% secure.

Your choices

You can request that your account and associated data be deleted at any time by contacting us at the email below.
