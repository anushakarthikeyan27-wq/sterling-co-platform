Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a professional for my project?

Go to "Find a Professional," then search or filter by specialty, location, and availability. Click any profile to view their portfolio, credentials, and reviews.

How do I request a quote?

On a professional's profile, click "Request a Quote," fill in your project details, and it's sent directly to them. You can track its status from your Dashboard under "Projects & Quotes."

Is my contact information visible to everyone?

No. A professional's contact details stay private and are only revealed once a project has been accepted.

How do I message a professional directly?

Click "Message" on their profile. This starts a real conversation you can continue from your Dashboard's Messages tab.

I'm a professional. How do I get listed?

Sign up and choose "I'm a Professional." Once verified, you can add your portfolio, credentials, and set your availability.

Does Sterling & Co take a commission?

Not currently — the platform is free to use while in active development.
